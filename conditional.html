Clicking a label sets:

The word given (or the bar |) tells you which row or column to zoom into. Everything outside it is ignored.

The given row or column total becomes the new bottom of the fraction. The top is the cell where the event and the given cross.

P(A | B) and P(B | A) have the same top number but different bottoms.
